VCP Scanner — Operation Manual
操作手冊 · Volatility Contraction Pattern Engine
System Overview

The Provest VCP Scanner screens 830+ tickers nightly using the Minervini Volatility Contraction Pattern methodology, enhanced with Pocket Pivot detection and Cup & Handle confirmation. It surfaces stocks where institutional accumulation is occurring and breakouts are imminent.

Important: Run the engine after 4:30 pm ET for confirmed end-of-day signals. Intraday runs may miss same-day breakouts (e.g. a C&H that triggers at close will show as T-1 if the engine ran at 2:30 pm).

Signal Types — Ranked by Setup Quality
☕ C&H
Cup & Handle Breakout — Highest Conviction Three conditions must align: (1) Standard BUY breakout, (2) ATR channel Squeeze within prior 3 bars, (3) Volume dry-up (<70% of 50-day avg) within prior 3 bars. The most precise setup — floating supply has been cleared and the base is fully formed.
💡 PP
Pocket Pivot — Pre-Breakout Accumulation Stock is still inside the base (below ATR upper band). Volume on this up-day exceeds the highest volume of any down-day in the past 10 bars. Signals institutional buying before the official breakout. Best used as an early entry with tight stop below the base.
BUY
BUY Breakout — Standard Momentum Entry Two consecutive closes above the ATR upper band (89-EMA ± 0.5× 89-period True Range EMA). Note: no volume requirement — check Vol Surge column to assess breakout quality.
SELL
SELL — Downtrend Confirmation Two consecutive closes below the ATR lower band. Exit longs and consider short or hedge positions for high-conviction setups.
EXIT
EXIT Neutral — Regime Change While in BULL regime, price closes below the ATR mid-band. The trend has stalled. Close long positions and wait for a new BUY signal.
Column Definitions
ColumnDefinition
SignalToday's VCP signal classification. Badge colour: green=BUY, amber=C&H, pink=PP, red=SELL. The day-badge (e.g. T-3) shows how many days ago this signal last fired if not today.
T-9 HistoryNine coloured dots showing the past 9 trading days (left = T-9, right = T-1). Larger dot = PP or C&H occurred that day. Hover any dot for date, signal type, strength, and volume multiple. See legend below the filter row for colour key.
SqueezeATR channel width vs 50-day average. ⏳ Xd = currently squeezing (channel narrower than 50-day avg) for X consecutive days. A longer squeeze = more compressed volatility = higher potential energy for a breakout.
Vol SurgeToday's volume ÷ 50-day average volume. 🔥 ≥1.5× HIGH, ⚡ 1.0–1.5× MED, ⚠️ <1.0× LOW. A C&H or BUY with HIGH vol is the most reliable setup.
PQRProvest Quant Ranking score (0–100). Combines price trend, volume strength, market regime, risk-adjusted return, and YTD growth. ≥80 = Leader, 65–79 = Developing, <50 = No Trade.
DecisionPQR engine classification. LEADER = top-tier, DEVELOPING = building momentum, NO TRADE = unfavourable conditions.
RegimeVCP-specific state: BULL = price above ATR upper band, BEAR = below lower band, NEUTRAL = inside channel. Different from PQR Regime (which uses SPY correlation).

T-N Lookback Slider

Default T-0 shows only tickers with your selected signal today. Slide to T-9 to include any ticker that had that signal within the past 9 trading days — useful for catching recent breakouts that may have continued higher.

Recommended Workflow

1. Check SPY Regime badge in the header. Only take long signals when SPY = BULL.

2. Filter Signal = C&H first. These are the cleanest setups. Sort by PQR descending.

3. Expand to PP. High PQR + PP + Squeeze = textbook VCP entry before the breakout.

4. Use T-9 Lookback to find recent breakouts still in the early stage of a move.

5. Click any ticker to open TradingView chart for visual confirmation.

系統概覽

Provest VCP 掃描器每晚對 830+ 個標的進行系統化掃描，結合 Minervini 波動率緊縮型態（VCP）方法論、口袋支點（Pocket Pivot）偵測，以及杯柄結構（Cup & Handle）確認，篩選出機構正在悄悄建倉、隨時可能引爆的標的。

重要提示：引擎應在 美股收盤後（美東時間 4:30pm 後）運行，以確保訊號基於當日確認收盤價。盤中運行可能遺漏當日觸發的訊號（例如：當天 C&H 在收盤時才確認，但引擎在 2:30pm 已跑完，則會顯示為 T-1）。

訊號類型 — 按設置質量排序
☕ C&H
杯柄突破 — 最高確信度訊號 三個條件必須同時符合：(1) 標準 BUY 突破，(2) 突破前 3 根 K 棒內出現過 ATR 通道緊縮（Squeeze），(3) 突破前 3 根 K 棒內出現過量縮（成交量低於 50 日均量的 70%）。浮額已被徹底洗清，籌碼結構最為乾淨。
💡 PP
口袋支點 — 正式突破前的機構吸籌訊號 股價仍在基地內（低於 ATR 上軌）。當日為陽線，且成交量超過過去 10 根 K 棒內所有陰線的最高成交量。代表主力資金在底部悄悄建倉。可作為提早介入點，止損設於基地低點。
BUY
BUY 突破 — 標準動能買入 連續兩根 K 棒收盤突破 ATR 通道上軌（89 EMA ± 0.5 倍 89 期真實波幅 EMA）。注意：BUY 本身不含量能要求，需查看成交量倍數欄位判斷突破強度。
SELL
SELL — 空頭確認 連續兩根 K 棒收盤跌破 ATR 通道下軌。清空多頭倉位，高確信度設置下可考慮做空或對沖。
EXIT
EXIT Neutral — 趨勢中止 處於多頭狀態時，收盤跌破 ATR 通道中線。趨勢已停滯，應平倉多頭並等待新的 BUY 訊號。
欄位說明
欄位說明
Signal 訊號當日 VCP 訊號分類。徽章顏色：綠色=BUY，琥珀=C&H，熒光粉=PP，紅色=SELL。如非今日訊號，會顯示 T-N 表示幾天前觸發。
T-9 History 歷史九個彩色方塊代表過去 9 個交易日（左邊最舊 T-9，右邊 T-1）。較大方塊表示當天出現 PP 或 C&H 訊號。懸停可查看日期、訊號類型、強度及成交量倍數。
Squeeze 緊縮ATR 通道寬度 vs 50 日均值。⏳ Xd = 目前正在緊縮（通道比 50 日均值更窄）且已持續 X 天。天數越長代表壓縮越充分，突破的爆發力理論上越強。
Vol Surge 量能今日成交量 ÷ 50 日均量。🔥 ≥1.5 倍 HIGH，⚡ 1.0–1.5 倍 MED，⚠️ <1.0 倍 LOW。C&H + HIGH 量能是最可靠的組合。
PQR 評分Provest 量化排名分數（0–100），整合價格趨勢、量能強度、市場環境、風險調整回報及年初至今增長。≥80 = 領導股，65–79 = 發展中，<50 = 不宜交易。
Decision 決策PQR 引擎分類。LEADER = 頂級，DEVELOPING = 建立動能，NO TRADE = 環境不佳。
Regime 狀態VCP 狀態機：BULL = 股價在 ATR 上軌以上，BEAR = 在下軌以下，NEUTRAL = 在通道內。

T-N 回溯滑桿

預設 T-0 只顯示今天有所選訊號的標的。向右拉至 T-9 可納入過去 9 個交易日內曾出現該訊號的標的，適合尋找近期突破但仍在初升段的機會。

建議操作流程

1. 先查看頂部 SPY Regime 徽章，僅在 SPY = BULL 時考慮做多。

2. 先篩選訊號 = C&H，這是最乾淨的設置，按 PQR 由高到低排序。

3. 再看 PP（口袋支點）。高 PQR + PP + Squeeze = 教科書級 VCP 提前佈局點。

4. 使用 T-9 回溯找尋最近幾天突破、仍在初升段的標的。

5. 點擊任何標的，直接跳轉 TradingView 圖表進行視覺確認。
